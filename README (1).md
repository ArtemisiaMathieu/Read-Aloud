# Read Aloud

Upload a PDF or EPUB and listen to it read aloud, right in your browser.

- Highlights each sentence as it's read
- Saves your place automatically and keeps your books for next time
- Pause, rewind or skip by sentence or paragraph, or tap any sentence to jump there
- Bookmarks, plus excerpts you can copy straight into a Google Doc
- Choose the voice and speed, with sepia, light or dark reading themes

Everything stays on your own device. Books are never uploaded anywhere.

**Tips:** Chrome and Edge usually have the most natural voices. On phones, keep the screen on while listening. Scanned PDFs (images of pages) and copy-protected store EPUBs can't be read.
